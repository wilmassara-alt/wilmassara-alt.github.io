Organisers say

What I believe

Young people lead now.

The "leaders of tomorrow" line is the most disagreed-with cliché in my work. 30% of Australians are under 25. They are not waiting.

Leadership is a core skill.

Maths and English are mandatory from day one. The skill every student uses for the rest of their life is left until the end, or left out.

Co-design, not for-design.

Decisions about young people without young people are tokenistic, even when well-intentioned. I say this as someone who has got it wrong.

Schools must embrace AI now.

Pretending students will figure it out later is the worst thing a school can do. This one gets pushback. I am fine with that.

Questions organisers ask

What does Wil Massara speak about?

Gen Z at work, staff retention and culture, youth leadership, education and co-design with young people. The three core keynotes are Lead Before You’re Ready, Gen Z at Work and Change Happens Together, plus the Creating Change workshop.

Who books Wil?

Corporate HR and leadership teams for staff conferences, culture days and offsites; conference organisers; schools and education systems; and local, state and federal government. Audiences range from executive teams to Year 5 students.

How much does it cost to book Wil Massara?

Fees depend on format, length, audience, location and date, and are quoted on request. Send the brief through the booking form and you will have a quote within one business day.

Does Wil travel internationally?

Yes. Wil is based in Sydney, Australia, has delivered keynotes in North America and virtually across Europe, and is booking dates in New Zealand, Asia, the UK and the US.

How long is a keynote?

Most keynotes run 30 to 60 minutes. Workshops run 90 minutes to a full day. Panels, MC roles and fireside conversations are also available.
