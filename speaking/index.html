Gen Z at Work

What the generation entering your workforce actually values, why they leave at month 14, and what to change about culture, feedback and leadership to keep them. From a speaker who is Gen Z.

Best for: Staff conferences and all-hands, HR and people leaders, Leadership offsites, Culture and engagement days, Graduate programs.

Lead Before You're Ready

The story of building an organisation that reached thousands of young people before the Year 12 results came back blank, and what that says about talent, age and who we back before they have the credentials.

Best for: Opening keynote, Staff conferences and all-hands, Industry summits, Youth advocacy events.

Change Happens Together

The decisions that stick are the ones made with the people they affect, not for them. A practical approach to intergenerational collaboration drawn from consulting work with government departments, councils and education systems.

Best for: Government and councils, Community organisations, Education systems, Corporate strategy days.

Questions organisers ask

How long does Wil speak for?

Keynotes run 30 to 60 minutes. Workshops run 90 minutes to a full day. Panels, fireside chats and MC roles are tailored to the program.

Does Wil tailor every keynote?

Yes. Every booking starts with a call to understand the audience, the outcome you want and the questions people in the room are already asking. Content, examples and even the title are adjusted.

What audiences does Wil speak to?

Students from Year 5 to Year 12, teachers and principals, corporate conferences and leadership teams, graduate cohorts, and government, council and youth-sector audiences.

How much does it cost to book Wil Massara?

Fees depend on format, length, audience, location and date, and are quoted on request. Send the brief through the booking form and you will have a quote within one business day.

Does Wil travel internationally?

Yes. Wil is based in Sydney, Australia, has delivered keynotes in North America and virtually across Europe, and is booking dates in New Zealand, Asia, the UK and the US.

How do I book?

Use the form on the booking page or email hello@wilmassara.com with the date, location, audience and format. You will hear back within one business day.
